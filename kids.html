Home / Kids Mode

A gentler Halo for children.

The same careful Halo, made for younger people: simple words, talking first, help that teaches rather than tells — and a grown-up in charge of anything that matters.

In development

Kids Mode isn’t in a public build yet. This page describes what we’re building. We’ll show real screenshots once a public build includes it, and we won’t describe safety features beyond what is built and tested.

What’s different

Its own space, not a recoloured grown-up app.

Talk first

A big microphone button, answers read aloud, and short sentences. Reading is welcome but never required.

Few choices at a time

At most three options on screen, large buttons, and one obvious next step.

A grown-up gate

Phone actions, the camera, permissions and settings always ask a grown-up first.

Homework help

Hints before answers.

A child can take a photo of their homework and ask for help. Halo guides them one small step at a time, so the understanding is theirs.

  1. 1“What is the question asking?”Halo reads the task aloud and checks it understood the photo.
  2. 2A small hintOne nudge in the right direction.
  3. 3Explain it another wayA simpler picture or a familiar example.
  4. 4“Let’s try again”The child answers. Mistakes are part of learning.
  5. 5Show the solution — only if askedAfter trying, with a clear “are you sure?” first.

Make and read

Stories to make, stories to hear.

Children can make a story or a picture with Halo, then listen to it read aloud with the words lighting up as they go. Encouragement is kind and optional — no points, no streaks, no pressure.

For grown-ups

What you can count on — and what we won’t claim.

What Kids Mode is designed to do

  • Ask a grown-up before any phone action, camera use or settings change
  • Keep advanced settings behind a grown-up lock
  • Use simple, kind language, and say “ask a grown-up if you’re not sure”
  • Teach with hints rather than handing over answers

What we don’t claim

  • That Kids Mode is finished or safe for unsupervised use
  • That any AI answer is always correct
  • That a provider you connect follows child-specific rules — check its policy
  • Any certification or regulatory approval
