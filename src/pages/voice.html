Home / Voice

Talk, listen, read along.

Voice isn’t a separate mode in Halo. You can speak or listen in Home, in your notes, on a Canvas and in Study — wherever typing or reading is harder than talking.

What the ring tells you

You can always see what Halo is doing with your voice.

  • {{RING:listening}}ListeningThe microphone is on and Halo hears you. Nothing is recorded before you start.
  • {{RING:capturing_voice}}Writing it downYour words appear on screen as you speak, so you can catch mistakes.
  • {{RING:speaking}}SpeakingHalo reads its answer — or your note — aloud.
  • {{RING:stopped}}StoppedPress Stop or start talking, and Halo stops straight away.

Voices on your phone

Local voices, when you install them.

You can download a voice pack so Halo speaks without sending text anywhere. You pick it, you test it, and Halo only uses a voice you’ve listened to and approved.

How well voice works depends on your phone, your language and which packs you install. Speech isn’t offline in every language, and speed varies by device.

Model names, for the curious

Speaking voice: Supertonic 3 (INT8). Alternative: Kokoro 1.1 (published pack: English and Chinese). Speech recognition: Whisper Small INT8 (Whisper Base INT8 is the lighter option). Speech activity: Silero VAD. Each is downloaded only when you choose, and a download alone doesn’t mean it works well on your phone.

Accessibility

Voice is for everyone who finds typing or reading hard.

That includes children, older people, people with low vision and anyone with their hands full. Text and voice always do the same things — neither is a lesser way to use Halo.
