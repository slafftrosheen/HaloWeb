Ask, act, know what happened

Halo asks before it acts — and tells you what really happened.

An assistant is only useful if you can trust it. So Halo checks in before anything that matters, and its Activity list never dresses up a guess as a success.

Privacy & price

Private by default. Clear when it isn’t.

  • {{I:on_device}}

    Stays on your phone

    • Your notes, files and memory
    • Answers from on-device models
    • Your settings and permissions
  • {{I:cloud}}

    Leaves only if you choose

    • Requests to an AI provider you set up
    • Requests to your own computer on your network
    • Under that provider’s own terms
  • {{I:no_account}}

    Halo itself doesn’t collect

    • No Halo account
    • No analytics or tracking
    • No Halo-run servers holding your data

No Halo subscription. A one-time Google Play price of about US$5 is being considered for a future release. The alpha on GitHub is free to try.
