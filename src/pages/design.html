Brand basics

Lowercase, open, mint.

The wordmark

Lowercase “halo” in Sora, with the open ring as the “o”. Never stretched, closed, recoloured or decorated.

Colour

Night Ink and Cream carry most of every layout; Halo Mint marks light and action; Sunrise Apricot is kept for warmth and “needs you”.

Type

Sora for headings, Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for reading — chosen to be easy for everyone.
