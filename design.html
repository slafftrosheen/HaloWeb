Home / Presence & brand

An open ring.

The gap is on purpose. Halo is never closed off: you can always see in, step in, or stop it. The same ring tells you what Halo is doing right now.

Presence states

Twelve states, each with one meaning.

These are the brand animations used in the app. Here they play once when they come into view; they are design files, not a live report from a phone.

  • IdleReady when you are.
  • ListeningThe microphone is on.
  • LookingUsing the camera you opened.
  • ThinkingWorking out an answer.
  • ActingDoing something you approved.
  • WaitingSent, waiting for a result.
  • Needs your OKNothing happens until you decide.
  • DoneOnly when the result was checked.
  • Heads-upSomething needs a look.
  • ProblemIt didn’t work; here’s one fix.
  • OfflineNo connection; local still works.
  • StoppedYou stopped it.
Brand basics

Lowercase, open, mint.

The wordmark

Lowercase “halo” in Sora, with the open ring as the “o”. Never stretched, closed, recoloured or decorated.

Colour

Night Ink and Cream carry most of every layout; Halo Mint marks light and action; Sunrise Apricot is kept for warmth and “needs you”.

Type

Sora for headings, Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for reading — chosen to be easy for everyone.
