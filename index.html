    One runtime, many capabilities
    
          
    Devices join by what they can do.
    
          
    Halo evaluates capabilities — microphone, speaker, camera, display, buttons — not brands. The runtime supplies reasoning and the workspace; devices contribute senses and outputs.

    Local by architecture
    
          
    Where processing happens is a decision you can see.
    
          
    
            Halo does not require a Halo cloud. Core runtime, Notebook and local inference
            can remain on your device. Network providers and connected-device links are
            explicit capabilities you choose to configure — and responses return to the
            device where the interaction began.

    Beyond the phone
    
          
    Wearables extend Halo. They don't define it.
    
          
    The generalized wearable capability layer and experimental embodiments are active tracks. Everything here builds on the same phone-first runtime.
    
        
    
        
    
          
    
            
      
              
    • Wearable capability layer — devices join by capability, starting from the verified glasses track. In development
      • 
              
    • Halo Platform — a tracked base with a pan servo; your phone docks on top, running the full runtime. Experimental
      • 
              
    • Cross-host future — the runtime is host-agnostic; Linux and Windows are future targets. Planned
      •
