Halo is one persistent intelligence. Everything else is a capability around it.

  • Phones — the first host, a complete assistant on its own.
  • Models — replaceable reasoning backends, never the runtime itself.
  • Notebook — the persistent workspace; your captures, notes and memory.
  • Devices — glasses, earbuds, robots: capabilities that plug in and unplug.

    One runtime, many devices
    
          
    Devices join by what they can do.
    
          
    Halo evaluates capabilities — microphone, speaker, camera, display, buttons, motion — not brands. Any device that offers capabilities can participate.
    
        
    
        
    
          
          
    
            
    Glassescamera · microphone · speaker · buttons
    
            
    Earbudsmicrophone · speaker · touch
    
            
    Phonecamera · microphone · speaker · display · sensors · compute
    
            
    Robotsdrive · camera · safety telemetry
    
          
    
        
    
        
    Halo cares about capabilities, not logos.
    
      
    
    
    

    
    
    
      
    
        
    
          
    Local by architecture
    
          
    Privacy you can trace through the diagram.
    
          
    Not a slogan — a topology. Everything inside the box lives on your device. The only thing that ever crosses the line is a model request you explicitly configured.
    
        
    
        
    
          
        
    
        
    
          No Halo account. Nothing to sign into.
          No Halo cloud required. The runtime runs without it.
          No helper application. Native OS APIs first.
          Perpetual ownership. Works even if our services vanish.
        
    
      
    
    
    

    
    
    
      
    
        
    
          
    Interaction-origin continuity
    
          
    Answers return to where they began.
    
          
    If you speak through a wearable, Halo prefers that wearable for the response. Toggle a capability off and watch the fallback happen — deliberately and visibly.
