Your wearables are the senses. Your phone is the brain.

Halo is a cognitive layer for the wearables you already own — glasses, headphones, watches, health rings. It listens, sees, remembers and acts through an Android app, running its AI locally first, on your terms. No Halo cloud. No lock-in. Choose your own AI providers, or none at all.

How it works

One loop: capture, understand, remember, act

Halo is not a chatbot with a history — it is a cognitive workspace. Everything it hears or captures can flow through the same loop.

1

Capture

Speak, type, or press the button on your glasses. Photos, voice notes and conversations land in one place.

2

Understand

Speech-to-text, vision and reasoning run on your phone where possible — through the AI provider you choose.

3

Work

Ask questions, draft notes, set reminders, control your phone. Halo explains what it did and why.

4

Remember

What matters can be promoted to memory — explicitly, with provenance. Nothing is remembered behind your back.

5

Revisit

Notes, conversations and captures stay in your Notebook, searchable and exportable. Your data stays yours.

6

Act

Set a timer, call a contact, capture on the go. Every action shows its result — no silent side effects.

Privacy & local-first

Your data never has to leave your phone

Halo is built local-first: on-device processing wins whenever it can. When a task does need a bigger model, you decide which provider gets it.

Runs on your phone

Wake word, speech recognition, and even full language models can run locally on Android. No Halo-operated cloud exists.

Choose your providers

Connect OpenRouter, Ollama, or local llama.cpp models — or none. Halo works without any AI configured.

Own your data

Notes, conversations and memories are exportable in open formats. You can inspect, correct, and delete what Halo remembers.

Notebook

A workspace that remembers your context

Notebook is Halo's persistent workspace for notes and captures — the part of Halo that keeps working when the conversation ends.

Performance goals — targets under test, not measured results: wake latency <150 ms · local answer <1 s · >80% of system tasks fully offline.

Halo Platform

A tracked base that carries Halo around

The Halo Platform is a motorised base with tank-style tracks and a pan servo. Your phone docks on top — running the full Halo runtime — and drives the platform, so your assistant can physically come to you, face you, and see the room.

Phone docks, Halo drives

Your phone snaps onto the platform and connects over Bluetooth/Wi-Fi. The docked app runs Halo as usual — plus a drive surface for the base.

Tracks + pan servo

Tank-style tracks handle floors and thresholds; the pan servo turns Halo to face you while it talks, listens, or captures.

Safety comes standard

Borrowing the BuddyBot contract: motors stay disarmed until armed, motion is bounded and throttled, release means stop, and telemetry on screen stays fresh or the drive stops.
