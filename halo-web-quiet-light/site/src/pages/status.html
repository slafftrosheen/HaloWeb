What’s real today.

Three lists, kept honest. No progress bars, no launch dates — just where each part stands, with links to the evidence.

Last checked against the source on 19 October 2026. Latest public build: Android v0.1.0-alpha.2 (pre-release, 15 Oct 2026).

{{STATUS:available|Available in the Android alpha}}

Why this order

Trust first, gadgets later.

An assistant that controls your phone has to be trustworthy before it’s everywhere. So we’re building three things first: AI you control, actions that report honestly, and setup that anyone can follow. More devices come after that.
