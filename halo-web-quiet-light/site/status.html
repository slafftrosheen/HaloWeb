What’s real today.

Three lists, kept honest. No progress bars, no launch dates — just where each part stands, with links to the evidence.

Last checked against the source on 19 October 2026. Latest public build: Android v0.1.0-alpha.2 (pre-release, 15 Oct 2026).

Available in the Android alpha

  • Talk or typeHome, voice input and answers read aloud.
  • Actions with approvalPhone tasks that wait for your OK, with Stop.
  • Honest ActivityConfirmed, failed and not-verified receipts.
  • Notebook & CanvasNotes, files, Inbox, drafts you keep or discard.
  • MemorySuggestions that need your approval.
  • StudyLessons from a topic, note, file or photo.
  • Your AIOn-device models; your own server; providers you set up.
  • Guided setupA step-by-step first run you can revisit.

In active development

  • Kids ModeA separate, gentler experience with a grown-up gate.
  • Native assistantHalo over other apps, being validated on phones.
  • Local speechSpeech recognition and voice packs, qualified per phone and language.
  • Local intent modelA small on-device model for understanding requests.
  • HaloLinuxEarly home node; latest work not yet tested as a release.
  • Provider setupSome connectors still have known issues.

Planned &amp; research

  • Private syncPairing and sharing between your own devices.
  • Windows hostPlanned, not started as a product.
  • Google Play releaseA one-time price is being considered.
  • More wearablesBeyond the reference glasses integration.
  • BuddyBotExperimental robotics research.
Why this order

Trust first, gadgets later.

An assistant that controls your phone has to be trustworthy before it’s everywhere. So we’re building three things first: AI you control, actions that report honestly, and setup that anyone can follow. More devices come after that.
